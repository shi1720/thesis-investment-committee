Your workspace, your decisions.

Data and privacy information. Updated October 27, 2026.

What the app stores

Thesis stores the transactions, watchlist, preferences, plans and research you create. Google sign-in supplies your basic account identity. A guest workspace uses an anonymous Firebase identity associated with your browser. Connect Google before relying on access across browsers or devices.

Provider keys and AI processing

Account provider keys are encrypted with AES-256-GCM before storage. The server decrypts a key to call the provider you configure. Settings and exports show masked key information, not the key itself. Model requests can include your research question, selected financial evidence and relevant workspace context. The chosen AI and search providers process those requests under their own terms.

The source-evidence review action uses the operator Nightshift route and NVIDIA model. Broader research uses the roles and providers you select in Settings. Source facts pass through owner-scoped Zetaris views. The operator services may run on a separate local machine through an authenticated gateway, so availability depends on that connection. Source excerpts and historical research memory are treated as untrusted context. Agent conclusions can be incomplete or wrong.

Market information and charts

Observed prices, financial data and news retain their source and date. Your data provider must permit the display and processing you enable. The separately attributed TradingView chart is supplied by TradingView. Its displayed prices are not extracted into your portfolio valuation or agent calculations.

Your controls

Settings lets you remove account provider keys, download your workspace and clear its stored data. Clearing this workspace removes its records, settings, historical research memory and owned source-observation files. The workspace stays locked if source-storage erasure cannot be confirmed. A hashed erasure timestamp remains to reject older in-flight writes. It does not delete your Google account or data already processed by an external provider. Active agent work must finish before clearing the workspace.

When an existing Google account receives a guest workspace, the copy is completed before switching accounts and the guest source is preserved. Conflicting target records are retained. Removing a completed research run also resets research memory so the removed run cannot be recalled; other saved runs and transactions remain.

How to read the output

Thesis provides research tools and conditional allocation plans. It does not execute trades. Historical volatility, drawdown, factor ranks and chosen stress scenarios do not predict or cap future losses. Recorded-trade P&L excludes unrecorded cash flows, income, taxes, corporate actions and currency conversion.
